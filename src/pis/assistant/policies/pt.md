# Portugal

## Vacation (Portugal) {#vacation-pt}
Full-time employees in Portugal have 22 working days of vacation a year; part-time employees have the same share of their weekly hours. Unused days may be taken until 30 April of the following year. Request vacation in HiBob under Time off.

## Sick leave (Portugal) {#sick-pt}
If you are sick, tell your manager and record the absence in HiBob on the first day. Send the sickness certificate (certificado de incapacidade temporária) to the People team within five working days.

## Parental leave (Portugal) {#parental-pt}
Initial parental leave is 120 or 150 consecutive days, shared between the parents, paid by Social Security. Tell the People team 30 days before it starts.
