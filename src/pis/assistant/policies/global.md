# Company handbook (all countries)

This handbook belongs to the simulated Example Company. It is invented for this project.

## Payslips and pay day {#payday}
Salaries are paid monthly, on the last working day of the month. Payslips are available in the payroll provider's portal from the 25th. If your payslip looks wrong, raise it with the People team before the 5th of the following month so a correction can go into the next run.
Asked as: payday, pay date, salary payment date, when is pay day.

## Salary reviews {#salary-review}
Salaries are reviewed once a year in the April cycle. Changes approved in the cycle take effect on 1 April. Your manager explains the outcome to you in May. Pay bands are published internally for every level and country.

## Remote work and workations {#remote}
We are remote-first. You may work from anywhere in your country of employment. You may also work from another EU country for up to 30 working days a year (a workation), if you register the dates with the People team at least two weeks before you travel.

## Home office setup {#home-office}
Every new employee receives a one-time home office budget of 500 EUR for desk, chair and screen. Submit receipts through the expense tool within three months of your start date.

## Expenses {#expenses}
Submit work expenses through the expense tool within 30 days, with a receipt. Travel needs your manager's approval before you book. Expenses are reimbursed with the next salary payment.

## Learning budget {#learning}
Every employee has a learning budget of 1,000 EUR a year for courses, books and conferences, and access to LinkedIn Learning. Agree courses above 300 EUR with your manager first.

## Changing your personal details {#personal-details}
You can change your address, bank account and emergency contact yourself in HiBob under My profile. Changes to your name or tax details need a document; upload it in HiBob and the People team updates payroll.

## Leaving the company {#leaving}
Resign in writing to your manager and the People team. Your notice period is in your contract. Unused vacation is paid out or taken during the notice period, depending on your country's rules.

## Raising a concern {#concerns}
If you experience harassment, discrimination or anything that makes you feel unsafe, talk to your People partner or use the confidential whistleblowing channel. These conversations are handled by a person, never by the assistant.
