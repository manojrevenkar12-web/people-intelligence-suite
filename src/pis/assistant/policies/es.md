# Spain

## Vacation (Spain) {#vacation-es}
Full-time employees in Spain have 23 working days of vacation a year; part-time employees have the same share of their weekly hours. Vacation does not carry over unless you could not take it because of sick leave or parental leave. Request vacation in HiBob under Time off.

## Sick leave (Spain) {#sick-es}
If you are sick, tell your manager and record the absence in HiBob on the first day. The medical leave certificate (baja médica) is sent electronically by the public health service; you do not need to forward it.

## Parental leave (Spain) {#parental-es}
Each parent has 19 weeks of paid birth and childcare leave, paid by Social Security. The first six weeks after the birth are taken straight away; tell the People team 15 days before the remaining weeks start.
