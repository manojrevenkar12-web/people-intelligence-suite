# Italy

## Vacation (Italy) {#vacation-it}
Full-time employees in Italy have 26 working days of vacation a year; part-time employees have the same share of their weekly hours. At least two weeks must be taken in the year they are earned; the rest within 18 months. Request vacation in HiBob under Time off.

## Sick leave (Italy) {#sick-it}
If you are sick, tell your manager and record the absence in HiBob on the first day. Your doctor sends the certificate to INPS; send the protocol number to the People team the same day.

## Parental leave (Italy) {#parental-it}
Maternity leave (congedo di maternità) lasts five months; fathers have ten working days of paternity leave. Optional parental leave (congedo parentale) can be taken later. Tell the People team as early as you can.
