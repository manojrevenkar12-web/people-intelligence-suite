# Germany

## Vacation (Germany) {#vacation-de}
Full-time employees in Germany have 30 vacation days a year; part-time employees have the same share of their weekly hours. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year and must be taken by 31 March. Request vacation in HiBob under Time off.

## Sick leave (Germany) {#sick-de}
If you are sick, tell your manager and record the absence in HiBob before 10:00 on the first day. From the fourth calendar day you need a doctor's certificate (eAU); your doctor sends it to the health insurer and the People team retrieves it.

## Parental leave (Germany) {#parental-de}
You can take Elternzeit of up to three years per child. Tell the People team in writing at least seven weeks before it starts. Parental allowance (Elterngeld) is paid by the state, not by the company.

## Public holidays (Germany) {#holidays-de}
Public holidays follow the federal state of your home address. HiBob shows the holiday calendar for your state.
